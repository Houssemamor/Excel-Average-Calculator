Excel Average Calculator

Calculate subject, unit, and semester averages from Excel files

Drag and drop your Excel file or click to upload

How it works:

  • Drag and drop an Excel file, or click to browse and upload
  • The calculator will process subjects and group them by unit and semester
  • Subject averages are calculated using the formula: Exam × 60% + (Devoir/Projet/TP) × 40%
  • Unit and semester averages are weighted by coefficients
  • Final overall average is calculated at the end
